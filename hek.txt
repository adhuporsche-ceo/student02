STUDENT WEBSITE - EXTRA FEATURE SUGGESTIONS
===========================================

HIGH PRIORITY
-------------
1. Placement Dashboard
   - Show placement progress, profile completion, applications, offers and deadlines.
   - Add upcoming interview and placement drive reminders.

2. Company and Job Drive Listings
   - Create a faculty-managed drive/job board with company, role, salary, eligibility,
     location, description, application deadline and vacancy details.
   - Allow students to filter jobs by batch, department, CGPA, skills and location.
   - Enforce eligibility automatically before students apply.

3. Resume Builder and PDF Export
   - Generate ATS-friendly resumes from submitted profile data.
   - Support multiple templates and downloadable PDF/Word resumes.
   - Let students choose which projects, skills and achievements appear.

4. Application Workflow
   - Add stages such as registered, shortlisting, test, interview, offer and rejected.
   - Send email or in-app notifications on stage changes.
   - Allow students to upload resume and supporting documents for each application.

5. Interview and Assessment Preparation
   - Add aptitude, coding and interview preparation resources.
   - Create mock tests with scores, answer explanations and progress reports.
   - Allow faculty to schedule mock interviews and assign interview mentors.

6. Faculty Placement Analytics
   - Show placement percentage, department-wise results and highest/average packages.
   - Add recruiter response, offer conversion and application funnel reports.
   - Export placement reports to PDF or Excel.

MEDIUM PRIORITY
---------------
7. Notifications and Calendar
   - Add email, SMS and in-app notifications for deadlines and interviews.
   - Provide an integrated placement calendar and downloadable calendar events.

8. Document Vault
   - Store resumes, ID proof, certificates and offer letters securely.
   - Verify document status as pending, approved or rejected.

9. Student and Faculty Messaging
   - Add a protected placement chat or announcement system.
   - Support application-specific questions and faculty replies.

10. Mock and Real Coding Tests
    - Integrate timed coding assessments with test cases and difficulty levels.
    - Track solved problems and skill growth over time.

11. Alumni Network
    - Display placement mentors and alumni by department or company.
    - Enable approved mentorship and referral requests.

12. Recruiter Portal
    - Give verified recruiters access to eligible submitted profiles.
    - Support shortlisting, interview scheduling and offer collection.

13. Profile Strength and Placement Readiness
    - Score profile completeness and suggest missing skills or projects.
    - Display readiness categories such as needs improvement, ready or placement-ready.

14. Referral and Recommendation System
    - Track referrals from faculty and alumni.
    - Record referral source and outcome for each application.

15. Accessibility and Mobile Experience
    - Improve keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels and high-contrast mode.
    - Make tables, forms and timelines fully usable on phones.

ADVANCED FEATURES
-----------------
16. AI Resume Review
    - Review resume structure, grammar, keywords and role alignment.
    - Generate improvement suggestions without exposing private student data.

17. AI Job Matching
    - Match student skills and CGPA with relevant company requirements.
    - Show matched, missing and recommended skills for every role.

18. AI Interview Practice
    - Run role-based mock interviews with voice/text answers and feedback.
    - Track confidence, response quality and repeated weaknesses.

19. Certificate and Offer Verification
    - Add upload, verification and tamper-evidence workflows.
    - Restrict sensitive documents to authorized students and faculty.

20. Audit and Privacy Controls
    - Log all profile, document, recruiter and faculty actions.
    - Add consent management and student-controlled profile visibility.

RECOMMENDED IMPLEMENTATION ORDER
--------------------------------
Phase 1: Placement dashboard, job drive listings, resume builder and deadline reminders.
Phase 2: Interview workflow, document vault, notifications and analytics.
Phase 3: Recruiter portal, coding tests, alumni mentoring and accessibility improvements.
Phase 4: AI resume review, AI job matching and AI interview practice.
